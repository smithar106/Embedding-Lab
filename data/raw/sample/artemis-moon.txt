Artemis Moon Program
The Artemis program is NASA's effort to return humans to the Moon and establish a long-term presence there. The first uncrewed mission tested the Space Launch System rocket and the Orion spacecraft in lunar orbit. Later missions are planned to land astronauts near the lunar south pole, a region where water ice is thought to exist in permanently shadowed craters. That ice could potentially be used to support crews and produce propellant. The program is a stepping stone toward future crewed missions to Mars, testing the technologies needed for deep-space exploration. Artemis focuses on the Moon and lunar exploration rather than the ancient history of Martian water.
