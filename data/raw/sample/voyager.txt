Voyager Interstellar Mission
NASA launched the twin Voyager spacecraft in 1977 to study the outer planets of the solar system. Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 flew past Jupiter and Saturn, returning the first detailed images of those worlds and their moons. Voyager 2 continued on to Uranus and Neptune, and remains the only spacecraft to have visited those ice giants. After completing their planetary encounters, both spacecraft continued outward and have now crossed into interstellar space, the region beyond the influence of the Sun's magnetic field. They carry golden records containing sounds and images of Earth, intended as a message to any extraterrestrial civilization that might find them. The Voyager mission is about exploration of the outer planets and deep space, not about the history of water on Mars.
